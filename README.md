## 🚨 Problem Statement
Over **3.5 billion people** live in regions with limited access to specialist healthcare. Clinicians in rural clinics make high-stakes diagnostic decisions without access to clinical guidelines or specialist consultation.

## 💡 Solution — MediGuide AI
An AI-powered clinical decision support agent that helps doctors in resource-limited settings worldwide make faster, safer clinical decisions.

- 🔍 **Analyzes** patient symptoms step by step
- 🧠 **Reasons** through differential diagnoses with probabilities
- 📋 **Recommends** WHO-guideline based treatment protocols
- 🚨 **Flags** emergency referral criteria
- 📚 **Cites** WHO, CDC sources for every recommendation
